# Losartan for hypertension

Losartan is an angiotensin II receptor blocker used for hypertension.

In this demo knowledge base, the matching indication is hypertension.
The demo recommended dose for hypertension is 50 mg once daily, oral.

Losartan may also be used for diabetic nephropathy in the demo knowledge base.
This note is educational evidence for retrieval. It is not clinical advice.
